WHY WE ARE HERE
---------------
Runtime logs proved:
  - face_skin_ok runs at char select (ownerLow=0) but NOT during in-world local
    RenderPrep with gear tint.
  - skin_layout (PasteSkinLayout hook) never fires in-world in recent logs.
  - Remotes look OK because native_first assemble runs face before gear tint.
  - Local self tints gear immediately → face mips never rebuilt → black face.
  - local_face_refresh (second RenderPrep) → invisible player (composite destroyed).

Root issue is NOT "missing one if-statement" — we do not fully understand the
client's section dispatch, scratch lifetime, and local-vs-remote CharComponent
paths. More RE before more hooks.
